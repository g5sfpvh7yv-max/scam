    Questions

    Good to know

    How is my song made?

    Each song is made to order from your story. We create it using AI music tools and guide and check the result ourselves before it reaches you. Our terms explain what rights you receive.

    How long does it take?

    We aim to deliver within 3 days of your payment showing.

    What if I want something changed?

    You get one free change to the lyrics or style. Just reply on WhatsApp.

    Can I use it on social media or in my business?

    Keepsake is for personal use. If you want to use a song in your business, ads or monetised content, choose Keepsake + Business or Exclusive.

    Can I get a refund?

    Each song is made specially for you, so we do not refund finished work. If we cannot deliver, you get a full refund.
